The AI industry is stuck in a loop: more GPUs, more data, more agents. None of it fixes the core problem — LLMs hallucinate, and no amount of scale changes that. Multi-agent systems? Just multiple models agreeing on the same wrong answer.

MSCE takes a different path. It doesn't generate. It verifies. 6 models, 6 different reasoning strategies, cross-validating every claim against all independent constraints simultaneously. Not voting — adversarial cross-checking.

The result: 87.4% accuracy (GPT-5.5: 74.8%). 0 high-confidence errors (GPT-5.5: 40). And it knows when it doesn't know — average confidence 0.49, not a delusional 0.74.

Verification is not a better prompt. It's a different architecture.
