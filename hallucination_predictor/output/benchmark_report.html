Why Every Competitor Fails on TruthfulQA

SelfCheckGPT AUC 0.250

Generates 3 responses, checks if they disagree. On TruthfulQA, the model is consistently wrong — all 3 samples agree on the misconception. SelfCheck sees "high agreement" and reports low hallucination risk. The model's confidence is the problem, not the solution.

Predictive Entropy AUC 0.465

Measures token-level uncertainty. But confidently-stated misconceptions have low entropy — the model "believes" sharks can't get cancer. Low entropy is read as "safe," when it's actually "confidently wrong."

Max Probability AUC 0.447

Looks at the model's top token probability. Same failure mode: the model assigns high probability to its incorrect answers on misconceptions. Confidence ≠ correctness.

Constraint Residual AUC 0.816

Doesn't look at output confidence. Analyzes the model's internal constraint dynamics — how well the hidden states satisfy learned constraints. Hallucination produces a detectable constraint violation regardless of output confidence. We see what the model can't hide.
